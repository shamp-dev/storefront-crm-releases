Questions

Do I need internet?

Only to download the app. Billing, stock and reports all work offline. Your shop keeps running when the internet is down.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer, not on the internet. The app makes a backup every day, and you can copy backups to a USB drive or a OneDrive or Google Drive folder.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. Install it on each computer. Make one the main computer in Settings → This computer, then connect the others to it. They all share the same sales, stock and customers.

Which barcode scanners and printers work?

Any USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner works without setup. You can also use a phone or tablet camera. Any printer installed in Windows works, including 80 mm thermal receipt printers and Zebra, Dymo or Brother label printers.

Can my staff use it without seeing profits?

Yes. Give staff a cashier login: they can bill and look up products, but they can't see cost prices, reports or settings.

Why does Windows show a warning?

Windows warns about new programs that aren't signed with an expensive certificate. Click More info → Run anyway. The app is the same either way.

How do I update?

Download the latest version from this page and run it. It installs over the old one, and your data is kept.
